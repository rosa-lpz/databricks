# Databricks Apps: Architecting Production-Ready Data & AI Apps

* Databricks Events - [Databricks Apps: Architecting Production-Ready Data & AI Apps](https://youtu.be/p3Z4K1BGaKE)

Databricks Apps is the production-ready platform for building data and AI applications directly on your enterprise data — no separate infrastructure, Unity Catalog governance built in, and authentication handled through Service Principals or OAuth. This video is a 75-minute technical deep dive covering every architectural layer developers face: framework selection (Streamlit/Gradio vs. full-stack JS/TS vs. compiled Go/Rust executables), Lakebase for OLTP state and database branching, agentic back-ends with MCP servers, auto-scaling with load-balanced replicas, CI/CD with Databricks Asset Bundles, and OpenTelemetry observability. 

Building internal apps on top of your data warehouse used to require 37 separate steps before you could write a single line of business logic. This video walks through how Databricks Apps collapses that into a governed, serverless platform — with live demos of MCP server integration, agentic workflows, automated testing strategies using dual-LLM patterns, and deployment pipelines. Roadmap topics covered include the upcoming Genie App Builder, serverless micro-VM pricing, and mobile support.
